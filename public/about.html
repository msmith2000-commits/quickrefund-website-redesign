About QuickRefund

Built by the Operators Who Wrote the Rules on Dispute Risk.

QuickRefund was founded by a team that has spent decades inside merchant acquiring, high-risk processing, and card network policy. We built this platform because we've seen — from every side of the table — what dispute ratios do to a merchant's business.

Our Story

Pre-Dispute Automation, Built by Payments Veterans.

QuickRefund intercepts disputes at the gateway level — server-to-server, against the original transaction — before they ever reach the banking system. No alerts. No chargebacks. No ratio damage.

That approach didn't come from a pitch deck. It came from decades of operating inside acquirers, processors, and Visa itself — seeing firsthand how dispute frameworks are built, where merchants fail them, and what actually moves the ratio.
