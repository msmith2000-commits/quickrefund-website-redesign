How It Works

QuickRefund Resolves Disputes Before They Enter the Banking System.

Most tools activate after a dispute reaches the bank. By then, the TC40 or TC15 is already filed and your ratio is already hit. QuickRefund works earlier — at the gateway level — so the dispute never exists.

01

We Connect Directly to Your Gateway

QuickRefund integrates server-to-server with your payment gateway (NMI, Authorize.net, Stripe, and others). We see every transaction at the source — not through a third-party feed, not through scraping, not through alerts. Direct gateway access. Setup takes approximately 5 minutes with admin credentials.

Server-to-server gateway integration
02

We Reach the Consumer Before Their Bank Does

After a transaction processes, QuickRefund sends branded, configurable messages (email and/or SMS) to the consumer with a secure one-click refund link. The consumer can resolve their issue directly — no phone calls, no bank, no dispute. The messaging is fully customizable: timing, frequency, and link expiration are all under your control.

Patent-pending one-click refund link
03

QR Chat Resolves Descriptor Confusion First

Many chargebacks happen because the consumer doesn't recognize the charge on their statement. QR Chat is an AI-powered conversational interface that helps consumers identify the transaction before they escalate. If the consumer recognizes the charge, no refund is needed. If they don't, the refund path is one click away. This alone eliminates a significant percentage of "unrecognized charge" disputes.

AI-powered descriptor resolution
04

You Stay in Control — Every Refund, Every Time

Merchants configure thresholds, timing controls, amount limits, and per-card restrictions. You decide what gets refunded automatically and what goes to a queue for review. No surprise refunds. No open-ended refund buttons. You can set dollar caps, time-based restrictions (e.g., no refunds in the first 7 days), and per-card limits to prevent abuse.

Full merchant control layer
05

Refunds Execute at the Gateway — Against the Original Transaction

This is the critical difference. Every QuickRefund refund goes server-to-server through your gateway, against the original transaction ID, back to the original payment method. One refund per transaction. No double-dips across tools or teams. The refund is tied to the exact transaction identity that alerts and chargebacks reference — so once it's closed, a chargeback on that transaction is not possible.

Closed-loop idempotent refunds
06

Your VAMP Ratio Stays Clean

Because the transaction is resolved before the consumer contacts their bank, no TC40 is filed, no TC15 is created, and nothing touches your VAMP ratio. The dispute simply never happens. Your acquirer sees improving metrics. Your reserves stay untouched. Your processing relationship is protected.

Zero ratio impact
Why This Can't Be Replicated

Refund Integrity. Not a Refund Button.

Merchants keep reducing this to "AI chat plus a refund button." The messaging is easy. The hard part is control and refund integrity — ensuring every refund is executed safely, tied to the original transaction, and auditable.

🔒

One Refund Per Transaction

Every refund is keyed to the original transaction ID. Idempotent by design — the same transaction cannot be refunded twice, even if multiple tools or teams are involved.

🏦

Original Payment Method Only

Refunds go back to the card that was charged. Not to a different account, not to store credit, not to a wallet. This is how Visa and Mastercard expect refunds to work.

🛡️

Trusted Third Party

QuickRefund operates as a neutral, trusted layer between the merchant and consumer. The consumer sees a branded, secure refund experience. The merchant gets auditable proof. Neither side can game the system.

📊

Full Audit Trail

Every refund action is logged with timestamp, transaction ID, consumer action, approval status, and outcome. Risk owners and acquirers can verify exactly what happened and when.

🎛️

Guardrails Against Over-Refunding

Thresholds, caps, throttles, and exception routing prevent runaway refunds. Merchants set the rules. QuickRefund enforces them at the gateway level.

📝

Remediation Reporting

QuickRefund generates compliance-ready remediation reports showing acquirers and networks exactly what prevention steps are in place. Documentation that risk teams trust.

"We Can Just Build a Refund Button Ourselves."

If a merchant says they can "just email a refund button," they have to answer one question: How will you execute a refund against the original transaction ID without gateway access?

The options are all bad: sending customers into the gateway UI (they can't authenticate), embedding credentials in an email (security nightmare), or calling the gateway API from the backend (now you need server-side permissions, idempotency, double-refund prevention, and audit logs).

If you're not refunding server-to-server through your gateway against the original transaction ID, back to the original payment method, you're not doing what we do. You're just sending money and hoping it prevents disputes.
